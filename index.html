The Solution

Three layers. Zero complexity.

Built on the IMF's three-layer framework for agentic AI in payments. AI handles orchestration; rules handle compliance; banks handle settlement.

1
Agentic AI

Orchestration Layer

AI agents analyze 15+ variables per transaction to select the optimal payment rail in real time, including cost, speed, success rate, and destination.

2
Rules-Based

Authorization Layer

KYC, AML, and local regulatory requirements for each jurisdiction enforced programmatically. No AI agent can bypass compliance controls.

3
Deterministic

Settlement Layer

Final settlement through regulated banking rails ensures legal finality. PayAgent orchestrates movement; licensed partners handle the money.
