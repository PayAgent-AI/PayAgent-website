Our Story

Building the Payment Rails LatAm Deserves.

PayAgent was founded on a simple insight: Latin America's 30 million SMEs deserve payment infrastructure as powerful as what enterprise companies get — but built for how they actually work.

The Mission

Every country in Latin America has its own payment rails, regulations, and consumer habits. For a small business trying to grow across borders, this fragmentation is a nightmare. PayAgent exists to collapse that complexity into one intelligent platform.

We use agentic AI — systems that can plan, reason, and execute multi-step financial tasks autonomously — to route every transaction through the optimal rail, reconcile everything automatically, and give business owners a single view of their money across every market.

Our Values

⚡

Infrastructure First

We build boring, reliable plumbing — not flashy features. The best payment system is one you never have to think about.

🌎

Local Depth

Every market is different. We go deep on local rails — PIX, SPEI, PSE, BRE-B — instead of treating LatAm as one thing.

🔒

Trust by Design

Compliance is hard-coded, not optional. KYC, AML, and local regulations are enforced programmatically on every transaction.

🤝

SME Obsessed
